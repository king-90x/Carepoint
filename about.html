A hospital built around the patient

CarePoint brings experienced doctors, modern facilities and honest communication together, so every family gets care they can trust.

Our Story

CarePoint started as a small clinic with a simple idea: good medical care should be close to home, clear to understand and fair to every patient.

Over the years we added specialities, an emergency unit and diagnostic services, always guided by the same purpose of treating people the way we would want our own family treated.

Thanks for trusting on us.

Our Mission

To provide safe, ethical and affordable healthcare, delivered by skilled professionals who listen to patients and involve them in every decision.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted hospital in our region, known for clinical quality, compassionate service and continuous improvement.
