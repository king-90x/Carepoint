  • Thyroid and lifestyle disorders

Cardiology

Prevention, diagnosis and treatment of heart and blood vessel conditions, with a focus on clear advice and long-term follow-up.

  • ECG and 2D Echo
  • Treadmill test

View Department Details

Emergency & Critical Care

108 / +91 79 0000 0000

Our emergency unit is open 24 hours, every day. If someone has severe chest pain, trouble breathing, heavy bleeding or an accident injury, call us or come straight in.

  • Trauma and accident care
  • Intensive care (ICU)
  • Ambulance support
  • Round-the-clock doctors
Location: Emergency entrance, ground floor, CarePoint Hospital, SG Highway, Ahmedabad.
Call Emergency

Department FAQs

If you are unsure, start with General Medicine. The physician will examine you and refer you to a specialist if needed. You can also call reception for guidance.

Appointments are recommended to reduce waiting time. Walk-in patients are seen as per doctor availability. Emergencies are always seen immediately.

Bring a photo ID, previous reports and prescriptions, and a list of medicines you are taking.
